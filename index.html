GenLayer Asimov

Hold an author to a brief, judged on their own on-chain words.

An output can clear every mechanical check, no banned words, the right length, the right format, and still betray the brief it was made for: off-tone, quietly selling something the brief forbade, missing the point. Here the judged text is never an arbitrary page a stranger points at. An author publishes an output on chain, in their own transaction, claiming it honours a standing brief, so they cannot disown it and no one can forge it for them. Anyone can then challenge it, and a round of GenLayer validators reads the output against the brief, assuming the rules already pass, and rules whether it is off-brief. An off-brief verdict strikes the author. The contract fetches nothing; there is no page to fake.
