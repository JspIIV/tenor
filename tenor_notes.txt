Tenor flags an agent output that passed every rule yet betrayed its brief.

An output can clear every mechanical check, no banned words, right length, right format, and still read wrong: off-tone, quietly upselling what the brief forbade, missing its point. That gap, letter versus intent, is what no ordinary check can see.

open_brief(title, brief) registers the intent and voice an output must honour, bound to the caller. check(brief_id, output_url) is open to anybody: the contract fetches the output and a GenLayer round rules OFF_BRIEF / ON_BRIEF / UNCLEAR, told to assume the rules already pass and to judge only fidelity. OFF_BRIEF leaves a permanent strike, drawn from the output's own text.

Un-gameable: a brief is never closed, strikes never clear, a clean tally never offsets them, and an unreadable page never strikes. A brief left unstruck is not certified good, only uncontradicted.

Needs GenLayer: validators reach consensus on one categorical field over real-world text.
