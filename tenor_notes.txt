Tenor holds an author to a brief, judged on their own on-chain words.

An output can clear every mechanical check, no banned words, right length, right format, and still betray the brief it was made for: off-tone, upselling what the brief forbade, missing its point. Letter versus intent is what no ordinary check can see.

open_brief(title, brief) states the intent and voice an output must honour. publish(brief_id, output) commits an output on chain; the author is the message sender, by construction, and the output is their own bytes. challenge(pub_id) is open to anybody: a GenLayer round reads the on-chain output against the brief, told to assume the rules pass, and returns OFF_BRIEF / ON_BRIEF / UNCLEAR. Only OFF_BRIEF strikes the author.

Provenance is settled by construction: the contract fetches nothing, so there is no page to forge, and no one can pin an output on an author who did not publish it. An author cannot disown a strike, clear it, or force a clean verdict. Un-gameable.
